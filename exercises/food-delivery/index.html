Food Delivery — Requirements Exercise

Design brief: Design an on-demand food delivery service that connects customers, restaurants, and delivery couriers.

Functional Requirements

  • Customers can search and browse nearby restaurants filtered by cuisine, price, rating, and estimated delivery time.
  • Customers can view a restaurant's menu, add items to a cart, customize items, and place an order with payment.
  • The system must show a live estimated delivery time before and after checkout.
  • Restaurants can accept, reject, or update the preparation status of incoming orders.
  • The system must assign each accepted order to an available courier based on proximity and current load.
  • Customers can track their order and the courier's location on a map in real time until delivery.
  • Couriers can view assigned pickups, mark items as picked up, and mark orders as delivered.
  • The system must send order lifecycle notifications (confirmed, preparing, out for delivery, delivered) to customers.
  • Customers can rate and review restaurants and couriers after an order completes.
  • The system must support promotions, discount codes, and surge pricing during peak demand.
  • Customers can view order history and re-order a previous order.
  • The system must handle cancellations and refunds subject to order state and policy.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Support 50M registered users with peaks of ~500K concurrent active orders during lunch and dinner windows.
  • Restaurant search and menu load must return within 200 ms at p95.
  • Courier location updates must propagate to the tracking customer within 3 seconds.
  • Order placement and payment flow must be highly available at 99.95% uptime.
  • Payment and order-state transitions must be strongly consistent; no double charges or lost orders.
  • Location and browse data may be eventually consistent within a few seconds.
  • Payment details and personal data (addresses, phone numbers) must be encrypted and PCI-compliant.
  • The system must scale geographically across multiple cities and regions with low regional latency.
  • Delivery-time estimates should be accurate within a ±5 minute band for most orders.
  • Operational cost per order must remain within a fixed budget as volume grows.

Assumptions & Out of Scope

  • Assume restaurant onboarding, menu ingestion, and courier recruitment are handled by separate internal tooling.
  • Assume a third-party mapping and routing provider supplies geocoding and travel-time data.
  • Grocery, pharmacy, and non-food logistics are out of scope for this exercise.
  • Courier payroll, tax reporting, and dispatcher back-office tools are excluded.
  • Assume a single supported currency per region and existing payment gateways.
