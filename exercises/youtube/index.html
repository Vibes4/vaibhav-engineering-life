YouTube — Requirements Exercise

Design brief: Design a video-sharing service where users can upload, store, and stream videos on demand.

Functional Requirements

  • Users can upload videos of varying sizes and formats.
  • Viewers can stream/play a video on demand across devices and network conditions.
  • The system must make uploaded videos playable at multiple resolutions/qualities.
  • Users can search for videos by title, description, and metadata.
  • Viewers can like/dislike, comment on, and share videos.
  • Users can subscribe to channels and see content from channels they follow.
  • The system must recommend videos to viewers.
  • The system must track and display per-video view counts and basic engagement metrics.
  • Creators can manage (edit metadata, set visibility, delete) their uploaded videos.
  • The system must generate thumbnails and support captions/subtitles.
  • The system must enforce content policy and allow flagging/removal of disallowed content.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Scale target: ~2B users, hundreds of hours of video uploaded per minute, and billions of views per day.
  • Heavily read-dominated: views vastly outnumber uploads.
  • Playback should start quickly, with time-to-first-frame p99 under ~1–2 seconds.
  • Streaming must adapt to the viewer's bandwidth to minimize buffering.
  • Availability target of 99.99% for the playback path.
  • Uploaded video content must be durably stored with very high durability (e.g., 99.999999999%).
  • Content must be delivered with low latency to a global audience.
  • The system must scale storage cost-effectively as the catalog grows continuously.
  • View counts and metadata can be eventually consistent; content availability should not be.
  • User data and unlisted/private content must be access-controlled.

Assumptions & Out of Scope

  • Focus on on-demand video; live streaming is out of scope unless stated.
  • Monetization, ads, and creator payouts are excluded.
  • Assume authentication and account management exist.
  • Detailed recommendation-model design is out of scope; treat recommendations as a capability requirement.
  • Copyright/content-ID matching internals are excluded beyond the flagging requirement.
