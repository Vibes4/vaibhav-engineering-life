Instagram — Requirements Exercise

Design brief: Design a photo- and video-sharing social service where users post media and view a personalized feed of content from accounts they follow.

Functional Requirements

  • Users can upload and publish photos and videos, optionally with captions and location tags.
  • Users can follow and unfollow other accounts.
  • Users can view a personalized home feed of posts from accounts they follow.
  • Users can like and comment on posts.
  • Users can view any user's profile and their published posts.
  • Users can search for accounts and discover content via hashtags or an explore surface.
  • The system must send notifications for follows, likes, comments, and mentions.
  • Users can post ephemeral stories that expire after a set period (e.g., 24 hours).
  • Users can send direct messages to other users.
  • Users can set an account to private so only approved followers see their content.
  • The system must display like counts, comment counts, and follower/following counts.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Scale target: ~1B+ users and hundreds of millions of posts uploaded per day.
  • Read-heavy workload: feed reads vastly outnumber writes (on the order of 100:1 or more).
  • Feed load and media rendering should be fast, with p99 feed latency under ~200 ms.
  • Availability target of 99.99% for feed and media serving.
  • Uploaded media must be stored durably and delivered globally with low latency.
  • Feeds and counts can be eventually consistent; a user must reliably see their own newly created post.
  • The system must handle celebrity accounts with tens of millions of followers (fan-out hot spots).
  • User privacy settings must be enforced consistently across feed, search, and direct access.
  • Cost of media storage and delivery must scale sub-linearly with growth where possible.

Assumptions & Out of Scope

  • Assume authentication and account management exist.
  • Ads, shopping, and monetization features are out of scope.
  • Live video and Reels-specific ranking are excluded unless stated.
  • Detailed recommendation/ranking model design is out of scope; treat feed ranking as a capability requirement.
  • Content moderation internals are excluded beyond basic reporting/takedown capability.
